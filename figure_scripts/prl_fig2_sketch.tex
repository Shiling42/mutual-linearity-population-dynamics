% Panel (c) of PRL Fig. 2 (TikZ): the geometry of a kink, Eq. (7) of the paper. Built by prl_fig2.py (needs the
% generated prl_fig2_layout.tex). Abundance space with x_e (the species that is lost) vertical; the horizontal plane
% x_e = 0 is where e is absent. The equilibrium comes down the old line (direction b_k) and reaches the plane at the
% kink (white circle, as in panel b). Continuing along b_k would make x_e negative (dashed); the new line stays in
% the plane, in the direction b_k' = b_k - (B_ek/B_ee) b_e: the tip of b_k is moved along b_e (the press response of
% the lost species) until it reaches the plane. The drawn vectors satisfy Eq. (7) exactly; grey arrow = predicted
% new direction, the same encoding as in panel (b).
